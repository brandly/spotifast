Sonos Connect: Visual Review

Idle Sonos receivers become normal transfer targets, without a receiver-login step. While a restricted Sonos is playing, transport and volume controls are enabled when local playback is ready. No layout, navigation, spacing, or theme redesign.

Baseline: 7048219 (Espaye's upstream base), with only the identical demo fixture added. Candidate: 34520a6, preserving Espaye's f61f0df. Actual captures: macOS ARM64, Retina 2x, 100% app zoom, demo playlist pl1, matching data and open picker state.

Scope: open idle picker and active restricted-device controls. Existing loading, empty, error, and ordinary receiver views are unchanged. Automated coverage checks the Office click action, session readiness, stale polls, sign-out, and queue ordering. Remaining checks: upstream CI on Linux/macOS/Windows and maintainer visual approval. Live playback was heard on the macOS Office Sonos Arc, including the build without the speculative Sonos HTTP login. Other speaker models, prolonged sleep, and a separate VLAN were not tested here.
